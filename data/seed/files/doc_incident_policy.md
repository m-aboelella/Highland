# Customer incident severity and communication policy

- Document ID: `doc_incident_policy`
- Type: `policy`
- Team: `support`
- Version: `2.2`
- Updated: `2026-04-05T10:00:00Z`

## Priority 1

<a id="pas_severity_p1"></a>
A production degradation affecting a primary customer workflow with no acceptable workaround is Priority 1. Provide an update at least every 60 minutes while actively mitigating.

## External updates

<a id="pas_incident_approval"></a>
Drafts generated from internal investigation notes require a human owner to verify customer-safe wording before they are posted to an external channel.
